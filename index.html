How it works

Four steps from first message to your slot.

01

Join a Zonity

Message us on WhatsApp to join the group for your locality. Access is members-only — through campaigns and join requests.

02

Get tied to your Zonity

We link your number to an allowed Zonity. Until that happens, we cannot take a pre-order for a slot.

03

Order when you want

As a member, message to place a pre-order for fruits or flowers in the next available window for your Zonity.

04

Receive in the window

Your order is fulfilled in that timed slot — special service for your Zonity, on a predictable schedule.

For members

Ordering is for known numbers only.

Zonited keeps a known list of member phone numbers, each tied to a Zonity. If you message to order and you are not on that list yet, we will guide you through the join workflow in WhatsApp — including tying you to an allowed Zonity — before taking a pre-order.

Already a member? Use Order on WhatsApp and tell us what you need for the next slot. New here? Start with Join a Zonity.

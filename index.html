How it works

Four steps from first message to your slot.

01

Request access

Message us on WhatsApp to join. Access is members-only — filled through campaigns and requests, not an open public catalog.

02

Get tied to a zonity

We link your number to an allowed zonity. Until that happens, we cannot take a pre-order for a slot.

03

Pre-order for a slot

When you are a member, message again to place a pre-order for fruits or flowers in the next available window for your zonity.

04

Receive in the window

Fulfillment happens in that timed slot for your zonity — concentrated and predictable for everyone nearby.

For members

Ordering is for known numbers only.

Zonited keeps a known list of member phone numbers, each tied to a zonity. If you message to order and you are not on that list yet, we will guide you through the join workflow in WhatsApp — including tying you to an allowed zonity — before taking a pre-order.

Already a member? Use Order on WhatsApp and tell us what you need for the next slot. New here? Start with Request access.

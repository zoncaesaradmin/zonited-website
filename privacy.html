Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zonited (“Zonited”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the website at zonited.com and conversations you start with us on WhatsApp for membership, zonity tying, and pre-orders.

1. Who we are

Zonited offers a members-oriented service for pre-ordering fruits and flowers for specific zonities (micro-localities) into fixed time slots. Contact for privacy questions:

  • WhatsApp: +91 83108 80446
  • Website: https://zonited.com/

2. Scope

This policy covers information we process when you:

  • Visit zonited.com or related Zonited web pages
  • Open a WhatsApp chat with us via links on this website
  • Request access, get tied to a zonity, or place a member pre-order through WhatsApp

3. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

  • Contact details — your WhatsApp phone number and the name shown on your WhatsApp profile
  • Membership and zonity details — whether you are on our member list, which zonity you are tied to, and related operational notes
  • Order details — items, quantities, slot preferences, and fulfillment notes you share in chat
  • Message content — the text you send us (including pre-filled join or order messages from this website)
  • Technical data — standard web server or hosting logs (for example IP address, browser type, and pages requested) if provided by our host

Our public website is static and does not require an account. We do not run a product catalog checkout on the site itself; ordering happens through WhatsApp.

4. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Respond to access requests and membership inquiries
  • Maintain a known member list and tie members to allowed zonities
  • Take and fulfill pre-orders for timed slots
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website and service communications
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us host the website or operate messaging (for example Cloudflare for hosting, and WhatsApp / Meta as the messaging platform you choose to contact us on). Those providers process information under their own terms and policies.

6. Retention

We keep membership, zonity, and order-related records only as long as needed for operations, dispute handling, and legal requirements, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

7. Your choices

You can stop messaging us on WhatsApp at any time. To ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of membership or order-related information we hold, contact us on the WhatsApp number above and describe your request.

8. Children

Zonited is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the website or WhatsApp contact after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, message us on WhatsApp at +91 83108 80446 or visit https://zonited.com/.

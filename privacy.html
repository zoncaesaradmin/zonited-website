Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zonited (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the static website at zonited.com and conversations you start with us on WhatsApp to learn about joining a Zonity or becoming a provider. A separate live app host at app.zonited.com may publish its own privacy terms when it launches.

1. Who we are

Zonited builds technology for Zonity — digitally connected neighborhood communities. Contact for privacy questions:

  • WhatsApp: +91 83108 80446
  • Website: https://zonited.com/

2. Scope

This policy covers information we process when you:

  • Visit zonited.com or related Zonited web pages
  • Open a WhatsApp chat with us via links on this website
  • Register interest to join a Zonity or become a provider through WhatsApp

3. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

  • Contact details — your WhatsApp phone number and the name shown on your WhatsApp profile
  • Interest details — whether you are registering as a resident or as a partner / operator / vendor, and any neighborhood or role notes you share
  • Message content — the text you send us (including pre-filled interest messages from this website)
  • Technical data — standard web server or hosting logs (for example IP address, browser type, and pages requested) if provided by our host

Our public website is static and does not require an account. We do not run a product checkout on the site itself; interest registration happens through WhatsApp.

4. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Respond to interest and partnership inquiries
  • Understand which neighborhoods or roles people are interested in
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website and service communications
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us host the website or operate messaging (for example Cloudflare for hosting, and WhatsApp / Meta as the messaging platform you choose to contact us on). Those providers process information under their own terms and policies.

6. Retention

We keep interest and conversation-related records only as long as needed for follow-up, operations, and legal requirements, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required.

7. Your choices

You can stop messaging us on WhatsApp at any time. To ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold, contact us on the WhatsApp number above and describe your request.

8. Children

Zonited is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the website or WhatsApp contact after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, message us on WhatsApp at +91 83108 80446 or visit https://zonited.com/.
